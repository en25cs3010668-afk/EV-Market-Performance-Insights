# Electric Vehicle Market Performance & Insights

**Data Analytics with Tableau – Capstone Project**

**Author:** Parvi Sharma, Medicaps University · **Team ID:** NA

A Tableau dashboard and story built on 100,000 electric vehicle records (`ev_market_test.csv`). It covers:
- EV sales by vehicle category and by market region
- the top manufacturers by sales and by customer rating
- battery technology use
- driving range and yearly CO₂ savings by category
- sales across model years 2018–2026

## Key findings
- **Categories:** Two-Wheeler Scooters sell the most (85.0 M units). Electric Buses sell the least (5.3 M units).
- **Regions:** sales are balanced across 7 market regions (108–113 M units each), with Europe slightly ahead.
- **Manufacturers:** SurgeWorks leads with 4.88 M units, but the top 10 manufacturers together hold only about 6% of sales.
- **Batteries:** NMC and LFP are used in about two-thirds of vehicles.
- **Range and CO₂:** Luxury Sedans have the longest range (549 km). Electric Buses, Pickup Trucks and Electric Trucks save the most CO₂ (about 9 t per vehicle per year).
